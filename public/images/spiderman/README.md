# Character artwork

`characters.webp`: 2172 × 724, RGBA, 391 KB. Three equal-width character stages used as a sprite sheet. Generated with the built-in image-generation tool, then encoded as WebP using Sharp. Generated artwork is approximate pose-matched imagery, not a rigged 3D morph.

Final generation prompt:

Create a transparent background character asset sheet for a fictional superhero website. Three equally sized side-by-side panels with the same fictional adult man in precisely the same pose, camera and scale, from head to mid thighs. He is looking down at his raised right hand, shown at chest height on viewer left. Panel 1: short dark hair, black formal suit, white shirt, light blue tie. Panel 2: halfway transformation into a black and red spider-themed superhero costume; some business suit still visible, red energy around hand. Panel 3: fully wearing the black and red superhero costume with a full face mask and white eye lenses. Maintain identical silhouette and position in all panels. Realistic cinematic 3D rendering, crimson rim light, detailed fabric. No words. Transparent background. Generous spacing between the three figures.
